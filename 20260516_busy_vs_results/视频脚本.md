# 视频脚本：勤奋的陷阱 — 为什么“特别忙但结果一般”的人决不能升？

## 0. 规格标准检查
- [x] 分镜数量：9 幕
- [x] 画幅：16:9 横版
- [x] 核心隐喻：深渊里的擦镜人
- [x] 技术点：组织熵增、信号噪音

---

## 分镜 1 — 建立世界观
- **时间（预估）**：0 ~ 8 秒
- **情绪档位**：1
- **旁白**：在一个永恒黑暗的海岛上，人们唯一赖以生存的，是一种极其精密的……“光能捕获仪”。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, a futuristic mysterious island in eternal darkness, a giant glowing complex light-capture machine at the center, industrial gothic style, dark atmospheric space at bottom.

## 分镜 2 — 勤奋的表象
- **时间（预估）**：8 ~ 15 秒
- **情绪档位**：1
- **旁白**：老木是岛上最勤奋的人。他每天工作 16 个小时，甚至在仪器旁……支起了行军床。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic medium shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, a middle-aged worker (Lao Mu) in worn-out overalls, leaning over the machine, a small military cot (camp bed) next to the giant machine, dimly lit, dusty environment, subject in upper third.

## 分镜 3 — 擦拭外壳
- **时间（预估）**：15 ~ 22 秒
- **情绪档位**：2
- **旁白**：他不分昼夜地擦拭捕获仪的外壳，直到它在微光下像镜子一样闪亮。周围的草地，连一粒灰尘……都没有。
- **画面描述**：Close up shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, hands vigorously polishing a metallic chrome surface of the machine, the surface reflects the worker's exhausted but determined face, extremely clean and shiny, contrast with the dark surroundings.

## 分镜 4 — 获得认可
- **时间（预估）**：22 ~ 30 秒
- **情绪档位**：2
- **旁白**：所有人都感叹：老木太拼了，他是全岛的楷模。领袖深受打动，决定提拔老木为……“首席维护官”。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, a crowd of silhouetted people looking up at the worker who stands on a platform in front of the machine, a leader figure handing him a golden badge, warm light spotlighting them, triumphant atmosphere.

## 分镜 5 — 突发意外
- **时间（预估）**：30 ~ 37 秒
- **情绪档位**：3
- **旁白**：然而，就在授衔的那天……全岛的灯火，瞬间……熄灭了。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, the island suddenly goes pitch black, the glowing machine's light flickers and dies, tiny silhouettes of people in panic, chaotic dark atmosphere, cold blue and black tones.

## 分镜 6 — 揭开真相
- **时间（预估）**：37 ~ 45 秒
- **情绪档位**：3
- **旁白**：人们惊恐地拆开仪器才发现：核心的感光元件，早已因为内部漏油而……彻底失效。
- **画面描述**：Extreme close up, 16:9 aspect ratio, the internal mechanism of the machine revealed, oily sludge leaking over a cracked glass sensor, rusted gears inside, contrast with the shiny exterior.

## 分镜 7 — 深刻讽刺
- **时间（预估）**：45 ~ 52 秒
- **情绪档位**：4
- **旁白**：而老木擦亮的，仅仅是那层与能量获取……毫无关系的……装饰性外壳。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic medium shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, Lao Mu holding a cleaning cloth, staring blankly at the broken internal parts, his reflection in the still-shiny outer casing is distorted, melancholy lighting.

## 分镜 8 — 组织洞察
- **时间（预估）**：52 ~ 62 秒
- **情绪档位**：1
- **旁白**：组织到底是奖励“结果”，还是奖励“表演”？当你奖励“擦外壳”的人，所有人都会放下扳手，转而去拿抹布。
- **画面描述**：Conceptual illustration, 16:9 aspect ratio, many workers holding cleaning cloths instead of tools, walking towards a pile of shiny but broken machines, minimalist and symbolic, dark background.

## 分镜 9 — 结论落位
- **时间（预估）**：62 ~ 70 秒
- **情绪档位**：4
- **旁白**：这就是“组织熵增”的起点。低效的勤奋，是对组织资源……最大的浪费。
- **画面描述**：Cinematic wide shot, 16:9 aspect ratio, a lone lighthouse or tower in the dark, the word "组织熵增" (Organizational Entropy) appearing as a faint glowing text in the sky, minimalist, professional design.
