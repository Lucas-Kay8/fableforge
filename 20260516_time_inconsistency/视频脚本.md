# Video Script: Time Inconsistency - The Fogport Lighthouse

## Topic
Time Inconsistency (Economic/Game Theory concept)
YouTube: https://youtu.be/cAJq7VN0hs0

## Narration (Full English)

1. **scene1**: In the coastal city of Fogport, survival depended on one rule: the lighthouse must burn every evening, without fail.
2. **scene2**: For generations, the flame was a promise. Sailors didn't need to see the rocks; they only needed to see the light.
3. **scene3**: Then came a clever ruler. He looked at the ledgers and saw only costs. "Why burn oil when the seas are calm?" he asked.
4. **scene4**: "If a ship appears, we'll light it then." He decided to delay. The first night, nothing happened. He saved oil, and felt brilliant.
5. **scene5**: Soon, the light became unpredictable. Some nights it was late; some nights, it stayed dark. The ledger looked beautiful.
6. **scene6**: But the sailors noticed. "Fogport is no longer reliable," they whispered. One by one, the ships began to steer elsewhere.
7. **scene7**: The harbor grew silent. The ruler was confused: "We only saved a little oil. Why has everyone vanished?"
8. **scene8**: Because a promise is only valuable when people believe it. Once you start calculating its cost, it is no longer a promise.
9. **scene9**: This is Time Inconsistency: what's optimal to promise today, becomes the first thing you want to betray tomorrow.
10. **scene10**: In leadership and life, your credibility isn't built on what you say, but on what you refuse to recalculate.
